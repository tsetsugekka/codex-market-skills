# Strategy Archetypes And Thesis-Aligned Trade Plans

Use this reference for daily or one-name recommendations and whenever the user asks how entry, stop, and exit should fit together.

The six ideas below are strategy archetypes, not six mutually exclusive stock species and not a promise of returns. Choose one primary archetype for the proposed position. A secondary tag is allowed only when it changes evidence gathering; the primary archetype still controls the plan.

## Shared Plan Contract

Before recommending a target, write the internal plan in this order:

1. `Return source`: what must create the gain—market normalization, repeated swing, cash distribution, event repricing, value convergence, or business growth.
2. `Entry`: observable conditions showing the thesis has started to be priced and the current risk/reward is acceptable.
3. `Execution invalidation`: a nearby price/structure level proving the current entry timing is wrong.
4. `Thesis invalidation`: evidence proving the investment premise is wrong. This may be slower than the execution stop.
5. `Time stop`: the deadline after which lack of progress weakens the thesis, especially for events and momentum.
6. `Exit`: realization condition derived from the same return source, plus partial-profit/trailing rules when appropriate.

Never justify holding with a different thesis after the original one fails. Cost basis is not a thesis. Do not use `越跌越买`, `做到负成本`, or a fixed percentage target without fresh evidence, position-risk limits, and explicit invalidation.

## 1. Crisis-Beta / Broad-Index Mean Reversion

- Vehicle: diversified broad index ETF or equivalent; avoid single-name survival risk and leveraged products by default.
- Entry: only after a genuine market-wide stress episode plus stabilization evidence such as breadth improvement, volatility retreat, selling exhaustion, or reclaim of a key level. A large fall alone is not confirmation.
- Invalidation: systemic conditions worsen, the panic low breaks without quick reclaim, or liquidity/credit stress keeps broadening.
- Exit: scale out as volatility and valuation/trend normalize; retain only if the market thesis has transitioned from mean reversion to a confirmed new trend.

## 2. Core-Quality Swing / Position Management

- Candidate: liquid, financially durable company with recurring evidence and a stable long-term thesis.
- Entry: tactical adds on supported pullbacks, breakout-retests, or renewed relative strength; keep the strategic core and tactical sleeve conceptually separate.
- Invalidation: tactical stop follows the swing structure; the core thesis fails only on material deterioration in earnings power, balance sheet, competitive position, or long-term trend.
- Exit: trim the tactical sleeve near resistance/overextension and rebuild only after a new setup. Never describe realized trading gains as making the remaining shares risk-free.

## 3. Dividend / Cash-Distribution Compounding

- Candidate: covered and durable dividend, resilient free cash flow, manageable leverage, and governance/capital-allocation quality. High yield alone may signal distress.
- Entry: yield and valuation are attractive relative to history and peers while dividend coverage is intact; avoid buying immediately before a likely cut or one-off special distribution.
- Invalidation: dividend cut risk rises, payout coverage or free cash flow deteriorates, leverage becomes unsafe, or the business loses earning power.
- Exit: income thesis deteriorates, valuation becomes excessive relative to growth/rates, or a superior risk-adjusted income alternative appears. Reinvestment is optional, not automatic.

## 4. Event-Driven Repricing

- Candidate: a dated, verifiable catalyst with a defined market expectation and asymmetric payoff.
- Entry: before the event only when probability, downside, and sizing are explicitly modeled; after the event only when the result beats expectations and price accepts it, or when a credible underreaction setup forms. `Event confirmed` does not mean `not priced in`.
- Invalidation: catalyst is delayed/fails, terms differ from the thesis, or price rejects the news and cannot reclaim the event level.
- Exit: at event realization, target repricing, or the predeclared time stop. Do not convert a failed event trade into an indefinite value hold.

## 5. Quality Value / Re-Rating

- Candidate: sound balance sheet and durable earning assets trading below a defensible value range, ideally with a credible re-rating catalyst. Cheap multiples without quality or a catalyst may be a value trap.
- Entry: margin of safety is measurable and deterioration has stabilized; staged buying must stop if the valuation inputs worsen.
- Invalidation: normalized earnings, asset value, balance sheet, governance, or catalyst assumptions deteriorate enough to reset fair value.
- Exit: price approaches the evidence-based fair-value range, the re-rating catalyst is exhausted, or the time allowed for convergence expires.

## 6. Growth / Optionality

- Candidate: large addressable market plus observable milestones in revenue quality, unit economics, margins, cash generation, product adoption, or competitive moat. A small market cap and a story are insufficient.
- Entry: milestone evidence and an inflection or confirmed trend justify the valuation; use smaller size when uncertainty or financing risk is high.
- Invalidation: milestone misses, slowing demand, worsening unit economics, dilution/financing stress, loss of competitive advantage, or a valuation thesis that no longer bridges to cash flow.
- Exit: milestones are met and valuation becomes extreme, the growth curve decelerates materially, or the thesis fails. Do not anchor to hoped-for multi-bagger outcomes.

## Recommendation Filter

- A long-term recommendation may use any archetype only when the expected return remains medium/long-term after short-term noise. Pure crisis-beta rebounds and pure event trades belong in tactical strategy unless a durable residual thesis remains after normalization or the event.
- Reject a candidate when entry is too far from a defensible invalidation level, the base-case upside does not compensate for downside, or the required exit depends on a different thesis.
- In the user-facing report, name the primary archetype in plain Chinese and include: `为什么现在进入`, `什么证明这次入场错了`, `什么证明长期逻辑错了`, and `在什么条件下退出`.
